常見問題

調音器沒有反應？請到「設定 → 喵喵練團趣」確認麥克風權限已開啟。
為什麼有些「音樂」App 的歌不能匯入？有版權保護（DRM）的歌曲，例如 Apple Music 串流的歌，無法匯入。只能匯入你自己擁有的檔案。
歌曲可以多長？目前可以匯入 7 分鐘以內的歌曲。
從 Google Drive 匯入找不到？請先安裝 Google Drive App，再到「檔案」App 的「瀏覽 → ⋯ → 編輯」把 Google Drive 打開。
去人聲要多久？一首 5 分鐘的歌大約 1 分鐘。較舊的機型會比較久，記憶體不足時也可能失敗；建議 iPhone 15 以上使用。
可以用翻譜踏板嗎？可以。藍牙翻譜踏板和鍵盤都支援：空白鍵播放／暫停、左右鍵快轉倒退、上下鍵（或 Page Up／Page Down）切換循環段落。
資料會上傳嗎？不會。所有歌曲和設定都只存在你的裝置裡。詳見隱私權政策。

FAQ

The tuner doesn't respond.Check that microphone access is on in Settings → HelloMusic.
Why can't I import some songs from the Music app?Copy-protected (DRM) songs, such as Apple Music streaming tracks, can't be imported. You can only import files you own.
How long can a song be?Songs up to 7 minutes can be imported.
Google Drive doesn't show up.Install the Google Drive app, then in the Files app go to Browse → ⋯ → Edit and turn Google Drive on.
How long does vocal removal take?About a minute for a 5-minute song. Older devices take longer and may fail if memory runs out; iPhone 15 or later is recommended.
Can I use a page-turner pedal?Yes. Bluetooth pedals and keyboards work: Space to play/pause, left/right to skip, up/down (or Page Up/Page Down) to switch loops.
Is my data uploaded?No. Everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
